Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Data Minimization

Minimal Browser is built with a "Privacy First" approach. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, browsing history, or search queries to our servers.

2. Local Storage

All your browsing data (History, Bookmarks, Passwords) is stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your phone unless you manually export it.

3. Third-Party Websites

When you visit websites, they may collect data according to their own privacy policies. Minimal Browser includes a built-in Ad-Blocker to reduce third-party tracking.

4. Permissions

The app only requests minimal permissions: Internet access (to browse) and Storage access (only if you choose to download files).
